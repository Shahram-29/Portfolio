# PredictPay — Smart AR Analytics Dashboard

Team project (Amjad Ali, Abdul Rehman, Shahram Sajawal) for Project Management for Business Analytics, M.Sc. Business Analytics, Dublin Business School.

An LSTM-based decision support system for B2B finance teams: automates accounts-receivable tracking, forecasts which customers will pay late, and is designed to integrate with Xero and Sage so a backlog of 50+ overdue invoices becomes a prioritised worklist.

Files: `PredictPay_Proposal.pptx`, `AR_Analytics_Dashboard_Proposal.pdf`.
