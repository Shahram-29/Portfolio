About me

I'm a business analytics graduate with a finance background, so I'm comfortable on both sides of the numbers: closing the books, running payroll, and reconciling accounts, then turning that same data into models and dashboards that explain what's actually happening in the business.

Recently I've been working on the data side of AI: evaluating and ranking large language model outputs against detailed rubrics, writing expert-level finance and accounting prompt–response pairs for fine-tuning, and documenting reasoning traces and error analysis for RLHF workflows. Careful, well-labelled data is the part most people skip, and it’s the part I enjoy. I use AI tools the same way I’d brief an analyst: with a reference example, the source documents attached, and a request to show the evaluation step by step rather than just the answer, then I iterate on what comes back.

I hold a First Class Honours M.Sc. in Business Analytics from Dublin Business School and a B.Sc. in Accounting & Finance from NUST. I'm based in Dublin and open to analytics, finance, and data roles across Ireland and remote.
